Liberty State Park / Pesin Dr Grove

Our nostalgic picnic spot from 2 years ago! Breathtaking views of the Statue of Liberty and Manhattan skyline.

Pesin Drive Picnic Grove

Liberty State Park, Jersey City, NJ 07305

Open Google Maps Pin
By Car / 車

Take NJ Turnpike or I-78 to Liberty State Park exit. Follow signs toward Morris Pesin Drive and picnic grove parking lots.

Transit / 公共交通

Take the Hudson-Bergen Light Rail to Liberty State Park Station, or NY Waterway ferry from Brookfield Place to LSP terminal.

Parking / 駐車場

Free/affordable parking lots available near Pesin Drive. Arrive by 11:00 AM to secure picnic tables together!

PARK AMENITIES / 設備

  • Built-in charcoal grills on site (bring your own charcoal & lighter fluid).
  • Spacious grassy lawns for soccer, frisbee, and cornhole games.
  • Restrooms and water fountains conveniently located nearby.
  • Unbeatable views of the Manhattan skyline & Statue of Liberty.
💡 Tip: Bring camping chairs for extra seating comfort!
